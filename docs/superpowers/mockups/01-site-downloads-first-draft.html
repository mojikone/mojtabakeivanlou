Where should the lock link live?

Click one. It only affects where a visitor finds the "Have an access code?" entry point.

1

Bottom of the Experience section (shown above)

Next to the projects it unlocks. Quiet, but a hirer you've emailed will find it where you tell them to look.

2

Inside the "o" menu, below the colour themes

Completely hidden from casual visitors. You'd tell the hirer "click the o in Mojtaba". Fewer strangers will ever see a lock.

3

No link at all — unlock through the address

You send the hirer a link like mojtabakeivanlou.com/#code=MK-7H2K-9QPD. The site unlocks itself on arrival. Nothing visible on the page, ever. Can be combined with 1 or 2.
