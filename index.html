About Me

Engineering water systems that communities depend on.

I'm a civil engineer specialising in hydraulics and water resources, currently leading complex infrastructure projects with Renardet S.A. & Partners in Muscat.

My career spans Iran, the United States, and Oman — giving me first-hand perspective on water challenges across vastly different climates, regulatory environments, and construction cultures.

I combine advanced numerical modelling (HEC-RAS 2D, InfoWorks ICM, Flow-3D) with GIS analysis and Python automation to deliver designs that are both technically rigorous and practically buildable.
